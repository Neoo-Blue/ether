# Ether v0.3.2

## Owner console

The console has been reorganized and reviewed page by page, including General, Apps, Integrations, Catalog, Quality and viewer management. Settings now follow the task they control, with clearer setup drawers, connection instructions and recovery actions. All owner-facing copy is translated into 20 languages.

- Manage Plex people, sign-in methods, sharing and viewer settings from the revised viewer pages.
- Set up poster providers independently, keeping credentials with each provider.
- Configure remote console access explicitly, with revised reverse-proxy guidance.
- Restore held Plex watch-state changes and retry failed items from the viewer page.
- Configure the identity Ether presents to Newznab and Torznab indexers.

## Playback and reliability

- Configurable Usenet read-ahead keeps a bounded window of the playing title in cache, with background priority and pause handling.
- Paused viewers no longer reduce the read-ahead allowance of active viewers.
- Playback quality rules take precedence over remote-connection preferences.
- Improved Plex subtitle handling, first-visit version selection and foreign-language audio selection.
- Improved handling of debrid provider refusals, indexer health-check volume, Live TV channel switches and idle CPU use.
- Filesystem compatibility fixes include Unraid and FreeBSD payload storage.
- Plex Web advanced filters, player settings and subtitle-download completion have been corrected.
- Owner collection rows now wait briefly for their first load, and cancelled NZB fetches no longer cause false indexer outage alerts.
- Visibility rules can hide titles from browsing while retaining search and playback access. The Everything else: Hide policy now applies consistently to browse and search.
- Collection URLs, zero-vote handling and the Now playing decision display have been corrected.

## Upgrade notes

- Existing poster-provider settings migrate to per-provider credentials; an active provider keeps its source and credentials.
- The old movie home-release gate is replaced by the New releases choice. When the old gate was enabled with Right away, it becomes On digital release. Rule browsing and visibility previews use the same cutoff as viewer browsing.
- Upcoming movies and episodes default to off for new configurations; explicit existing settings are preserved.
- Review General, Apps, Integrations, Catalog, Quality and viewer pages after upgrading.
- The plugin system remains planned for v0.3.3 and is not included in this release.
